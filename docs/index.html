Philosophy

Beyond Windows and Icons

What if we questioned everything we take for granted about desktop computing? These experiments explore alternative paradigms for human-computer interaction.

Frictionless

Remove the barriers between intention and action. Every interaction should feel natural and immediate.

Focused

Eliminate visual clutter and distractions. Your attention is precious and should be protected.

Fluid

Windows, apps, and content should flow and adapt to your needs, not force you into rigid structures.

Familiar

Innovation doesn't mean alienation. New ideas work with existing knowledge and muscle memory.

Core Ideas

Experiments in Interaction

Each script reimagines a fundamental aspect of desktop computing.

WindowScape

Stop arranging windows. The desktop tiles itself, the layout adapts to what you're doing, and minimized windows come back the way you left them.

^CmdL Cycle layouts

Palette

Run any menu item, open any file, calculate, search, launch. Drive the Mac by what you want, not by where it's filed.

^CmdSpace Or 5-finger trackpad tap

Muse

Ask an AI without leaving where you're typing. Whatever you had selected becomes context, and the answer drops in where your cursor was.

CmdCmd Double-tap right Command

EdgeHopper

Screen edges become portals. Push against an edge and your cursor wraps to the opposite side. Feels like magic, works like physics. Includes visual "membrane" feedback and drag-through support.

FrameMaster

Reclaim your screen edges. Hot corners become powerful actions. The menu bar and dock stay hidden until you need them. Every pixel serves your focus.

Corners: Close, Fullscreen, Minimize, Kill

ZXNav

Text navigation reimagined. Home, End, arrows, and editing keys move from far-flung corners to the row just below your thumbs. Hold Space and the bottom row becomes a navigation powerhouse.

SpaceZ Home SpaceX End

CloudPad

Your phone becomes a wireless keyboard and trackpad. A local web server turns any device with a browser into an input device for your Mac. Perfect for presentations or couch computing.

CmdCtrlC Copy server URL

Sssssssscroll

Scroll with your voice. A continuous "sssss" sound scrolls the page. Lip pops trigger actions. Hands-free navigation that feels surprisingly natural after a few minutes.

CmdAltCtrlS Toggle listener
